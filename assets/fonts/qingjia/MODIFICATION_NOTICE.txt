Modification date:
  2026-09-01

Changes:
  1. Glyph images were rasterized from the original outlines for the covered character set.
  2. Stroke skeletons were algorithmically extracted.
  3. Skeletons were expanded with angular diamond-shaped kernels.
  4. Small deterministic carving incisions and directional offsets were added.
  5. Resulting contours were polygonized and normalized to a 1000 UPM em square.
  6. Family names and metadata were changed.
  7. Ten original QingJia-themed private-use symbols were added at U+E000–U+E009.
  8. A new build system was created using fontTools.
